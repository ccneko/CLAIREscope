## 🧭 Key Walkthrough Milestones

1. **🎨 Static UMAP & Dynamic Contrast Tuning (`0:00 - 0:25`)**:
   - Dynamic colormap max reactivity synced with percentile thresholds and scale transformations.
   - Side-by-side multi-condition split with full-width bottom legend wrapping and high-contrast centroid labels.
2. **✨ Interactive 2D/3D WebGL Studio (`0:25 - 0:50`)**:
   - Real-time lasso sub-clustering, instant cluster auto-highlighting, and persistent camera orientations.
3. **🌿 Continuous Trajectory Kinetics (`0:50 - 1:15`)**:
   - Diffusion Pseudotime (DPT) spline modeling across differentiation branches with single-cell overlays.
4. **📈 Signature & Pathway Scoring (`1:15 - 1:35`)**:
   - Dynamic gene module expression computation and Mann-Whitney statistical ranking across conditions.
5. **📦 Bulk Figure & Tabular Export (`1:35 - 2:00`)**:
   - One-click ZIP generation containing publication-ready vector SVGs, 300-DPI PNGs, and structured CSV matrices.
